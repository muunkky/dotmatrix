# ASCII Art Pixels

## The Idea

**What**: Convert images into ASCII art where each "pixel" is a character from text (e.g., Pride and Prejudice excerpt). Characters vary in color, weight/boldness, and density to recreate the source image as readable text.

**Why**: Blends visual art with literature - creates posters where you can read a story AND see an image. Perfect for literary-themed art, book covers, or unique gifts. The text becomes the texture.
